Munich Quantum Valley · openQSE DOE booth dry run · 2026-11-11

About 60 seconds. ORNL just showed the openQSE stack; this is the same diagram, with MQV's part framed in blue. Everything above the blue frame is the openQSE reference implementation: VQE through StreamFlow and Slurm, Qiskit, QPM and QRI. At the QDMI line, openQSE loads one device: ours, mqss-qdmi-mqv, through MQT Core's QDMI driver. Behind that one device sits MQSS, the Munich Quantum Software Stack, which federates several machines: IQM at LRZ, and IQM on AWS through Braket. Three points: it is a standard interface, it installs with pip, and a new machine behind MQSS is just a new QPU name for users.
